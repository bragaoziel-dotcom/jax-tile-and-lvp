Builder tile out, porcelain in

Swapping builder ceramic for large-format porcelain sounds simple until the demo starts: production tile is often set thin over the slab with minimal prep, and getting it up without gouging the concrete is the slow part of the job. We remove the old floor, check the slab for cracks and hollow spots, and write the prep into the quote before setting starts — the new floor is only as flat and solid as what is under it.

Showers built to a price get re-tiled right

The most common Nocatee shower complaint is grout that keeps failing and walls that were never waterproofed the way a daily-use shower needs. A re-tile is the moment to fix what is behind the tile, not just the surface. Waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included only when it is part of the agreed tile scope; the specific system and any written warranty are confirmed on the quote.

Nocatee neighborhoods have HOA and architectural-review rules that can apply to exterior work and work hours; interior tile is normally straightforward, but tell us your village when you book the measure and we schedule inside the community's contractor hours. Nocatee tile questions

Tile installation in Nocatee — FAQs

Is there a published per-square-foot price for tile in Nocatee?

No. Tile is quoted after a free measure because the price depends on the tile size, the room, demolition and the prep the surface needs. The $3.99 per square foot figure on this website is only the waterproof LVP package, which also applies in Nocatee.

Can you replace the builder-grade tile in my Nocatee home?

Yes — that is the most common tile job in Nocatee. We remove the production ceramic or porcelain, check the slab for cracks and hollow spots, and write any prep into the quote before the new tile is set.

My shower grout keeps cracking. Is a re-tile the fix?

Usually, yes — recurring grout failure in a production shower often traces back to what is behind the tile. At the measure we look at the surround and say plainly whether a re-tile with proper waterproofing, confirmed on the quote, is the right scope.

Do you install large-format porcelain tile?

Yes. Large-format tile needs a flatter surface than standard tile, so slab flatness is checked at the measure and any leveling is listed in the written scope rather than discovered mid-job.

Do Nocatee HOA rules affect an interior tile job?

Interior tile work is normally not an architectural-review item, but communities set contractor hours and parking rules. Tell us your village when you book and we schedule the measure and the work inside those rules.
