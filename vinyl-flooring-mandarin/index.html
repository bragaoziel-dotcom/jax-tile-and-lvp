Two neighborhoods, two different jobs

Mandarin is old Jacksonville: oak-canopied streets along the St. Johns River, homes from the 1960s through the 90s, wood floors and tile that have lived a life. St. Johns — Julington Creek, Durbin Crossing, the CR-210 corridor — is the opposite: new subdivisions where the builder carpeted half the house. One page covers both because the package is the same, but the measure looks for different things in each, and this page says so plainly.

St. Johns: finishing what the builder started

In the CR-210 subdivisions the pattern is consistent — hard floor on the main living level, carpet in the bedrooms and on the stairs. Replacing the carpet with waterproof LVP is usually a multi-part quote: the rooms at the package price, carpet removal at $0.50/sq ft, a $400 dumpster when we haul debris, and the stairs as a per-step line item ($85–110/step). At the measure we compare in-stock colors to the builder floor under your light; an exact match to a discontinued product is not guaranteed, and a close tone at a doorway transition is the honest standard. More detail for Fruit Cove, Julington Creek, RiverTown and Beachwalk is on the St. Johns LVP page.

Mandarin: river air, older slabs, real wood

Mandarin homes sit closer to the river and the water table. Slabs here get a moisture check at the measure, and the Plus tier's moisture barrier is frequently the right call. Older Mandarin homes also have genuine wood floors — when the wood is sound, we will say refinishing beats covering it. When it is cupped, stained or patched with three decades of different boards, LVP over a flattened, checked surface is the practical answer. Either way you hear it at the measure, not after signing.

Stairs, halls and the connected-floor look

The reason most of these projects start is visual: one continuous floor from the entry through the halls. Halls are part of the package area; stairs are the separate line item above; transitions between the new LVP and rooms that keep tile or wood run $35–45 each and are counted at the measure. The written quote shows the full map before install day.

LVP in Mandarin & St. Johns — FAQs

Does the $3.99/sq ft LVP package apply in Mandarin and St. Johns?

Yes — the same package prices apply in Mandarin (32223, 32258) and the St. Johns area along CR-210 (32259). For 1,000 square feet and up, $3.99 per square foot includes waterproof LVP from the in-stock colors, installation and standard cleanup. Carpet removal is $0.50/sq ft (not included); dumpster is $400 when we haul debris. Plus is $4.49 per square foot and adds shoe molding and a moisture barrier. Premium starts at $4.99 per square foot for a thicker plank.

My St. Johns home is new construction. Why replace the builder carpet already?

It is the most common job on this page. Builders carpet bedrooms and stairs while the main level gets a hard floor, and the carpet shows wear years before the rest of the house does. Waterproof LVP in a tone close to the main floor ties the home together — samples are compared against your existing floor at the free measure.

Does the package include the stairs?

Stairs are quoted separately at $85–110 per step, because stair treads and nosing are cut work, not field planks. The number of steps and the price are on the written quote — no per-step surprises on install day.

Mandarin homes near the river deal with moisture. Is LVP still a good choice?

The wear surface is waterproof, which handles tracked-in water and humidity at the surface. What matters is the slab: low-lying Mandarin lots can have slabs that pass more moisture vapor. The Plus package adds a moisture barrier, and the free measure includes the slab check that decides whether your floor needs it.

Can LVP replace the wood floor in my older Mandarin home?

It can, and the honest conversation is about why. If the wood is worn but solid, refinishing may be the better investment; if it is water-damaged, cupped or you want a waterproof surface for a mudroom entry off the river side, LVP over a checked, flat surface is a practical swap. We will tell you which one your floor actually calls for at the measure.

What if my project is under 1,000 square feet?

The $3.99 package and the Plus and Premium prices are for projects of 1,000 sq ft and up. For smaller areas, call or text us and we will tell you whether we can schedule it.
