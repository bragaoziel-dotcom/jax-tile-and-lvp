St. Augustine homes are not one kind of floor job

St. Augustine mixes 1920s bungalows near the historic district, mid-century ranch homes, World Golf Village subdivisions and beach condos on Anastasia Island. Those houses sit on different foundations — raised wood subfloors in the older streets, concrete slab in the subdivisions — and floating LVP behaves differently on each. The free measure exists for exactly that: we check what is under your current floor before a written quote puts a package price on it.

Raised wood subfloors: the St. Augustine difference

On a concrete slab, the checks are flatness and moisture. On a raised wood subfloor — common in Lincolnville, North City and the older streets west of the bayfront — the checks are different: flex underfoot, squeaks, and boards softened by old plumbing leaks. Floating plank over a flexing subfloor opens joints. If sections need repair before the floor goes down, the repair is itemized on the quote. Skipping that step is how cheap installs fail in old houses.

Rentals, beach homes and turn cleanings

St. Augustine has a deep bench of vacation rentals and second homes. Waterproof LVP suits them: guests track in sand and water, turn cleanings are fast damp mops, and the wear layer takes rolling luggage better than carpet does. For a rental, the practical questions are scheduling between stays and plank replacement down the road — click-lock floors let a damaged section be swapped without replacing the room. Tell us the property is a rental on the form and we plan the install window around your bookings.

Condos on Anastasia Island

Beach condos add one layer: the association. Many buildings require a sound-rated underlayment under hard-surface floors and restrict work hours. That is one reason the Plus package, with its moisture barrier and underlayment options confirmed at the measure, is the usual starting point for condo work. Bring the association's flooring rules to the measure and we confirm the package against them before anything is ordered.

LVP in St. Augustine — FAQs

Does the $3.99/sq ft LVP package apply in St. Augustine?

Yes — the same package prices apply in St. Augustine. What if my St. Augustine project is under 500 square feet?

Smaller areas use a flat price instead of the per-square-foot packages: up to 150 square feet $895, 151 to 250 square feet $1,195, 251 to 350 square feet $1,495, and 351 to 499 square feet $1,895. The tier is confirmed at the free measure. Carpet removal and in-stock waterproof LVP are included.

Can LVP go over the wood subfloor in my older St. Augustine home?

Often yes. Many older St. Augustine homes sit on wood subfloors instead of concrete slab. At the free measure we check the subfloor for flex, squeaks and moisture damage. Soft or rotted sections have to be repaired before planks go down — that repair is quoted separately and listed on the written quote, never added silently.

Is waterproof LVP a good fit for a St. Augustine rental or Airbnb?

Yes. Short-term rental floors take luggage wheels, sandy shoes and frequent turn cleanings. Waterproof LVP handles spills and wet footprints on the surface instead of soaking in, and single damaged planks in a click-lock floor can be replaced without re-doing the whole room.

Can LVP be installed in a St. Augustine Beach condo?

It depends on the building. Some condo associations require a sound-rated underlayment under hard floors, and some set install hours. Send us the association rules with your request and we confirm at the measure whether the package fits the building before quoting.

Do you remove the old floor first?

Carpet and pad removal and haul-off are included in the package. Tile or glued-down floor removal is quoted separately ($2.00–2.50/sq ft), and the "existing floor" field on the form tells us what we are walking into.
