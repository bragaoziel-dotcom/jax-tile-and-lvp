Porcelain or ceramic: where each makes sense

Porcelain is denser and harder than standard ceramic, which is why it is the default for floors and wet areas. Ceramic wall tile is a solid choice for backsplashes and dry walls, where hardness matters less. Either way, the decision is about the room, not the brand name.

Tile size, pattern and layout

Large-format tile means fewer grout lines and a calmer floor, but it also demands a flatter surface. Smaller mosaics conform to slopes, which is why they are standard on shower floors. Layout matters too: straight lay reads clean, running bond hides slight size variation, and herringbone or diagonal patterns take more cuts and more time. Layout is planned before the first cut so cuts land in sensible places.

Grout basics and upkeep

Wide joints get sanded grout, narrow joints get unsanded grout — the joint width decides, not preference. Grout is sealed after it cures, and resealing is part of normal maintenance. For existing floors, re-grouting is assessed at the measure: when grout is failing across the whole floor, a full re-tile is often the honest answer rather than patching.

Tile installation FAQs

Is there a published price per square foot for tile?

No. Tile is quoted after a free measure because the price depends on the tile size, the room, demolition and the prep the surface needs. The $3.99 per square foot figure on this website is only the waterproof LVP package.

How is a tile quote different from the LVP package?

The LVP package is a published price for in-stock waterproof plank, installation, carpet removal and cleanup. Tile is a separate specialty on the same company, with its own written quote. You can request either one from this site.

Do shower projects include waterproofing?

A shower is not treated like a dry wall. Waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included only when it is part of the agreed tile scope. The specific system and any written warranty are confirmed on the quote.

Porcelain vs ceramic — which should I choose?

Porcelain for floors and wet areas: it is denser, harder and absorbs less water. Ceramic is fine for backsplashes and dry walls, where hardness matters less. We confirm the right body tile for your room at the measure.

Can you match tile I already have for a repair?

Sometimes, but matching discontinued tile is unreliable — dye lots and sizes vary between production runs. We look at the existing tile at the measure and tell you honestly whether a repair or a re-tile is the better call.

Do you re-grout existing floors?

It depends on the condition of the grout and tile. We assess it at the free measure — when grout is failing across the whole floor, a full re-tile is usually the better investment than patching.

How do I clean and maintain tile and grout?

Sweep and mop tile with a pH-neutral cleaner; avoid acidic cleaners on natural stone and on grout. Sealed grout stays cleaner longer, and resealing is normal maintenance every few years in wet areas.

How long will my tile job take?

It depends on the scope: a floor or backsplash is typically measured in days, a shower longer. The written quote lists the install dates before any work starts.
