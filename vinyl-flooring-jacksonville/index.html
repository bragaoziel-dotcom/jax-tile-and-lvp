Jacksonville LVP installation built around the existing floor

A vinyl plank floor can look simple once it is finished, but the quality of the result depends heavily on what happens underneath it. Before installation, we look at the existing flooring, transitions, door clearances, floor flatness and the areas where the new floor meets tile, carpet or exterior doors.

Jax Tile & LVP focuses on installation rather than selling a one-size-fits-all flooring package. If you already selected your LVP, we can quote the labor and preparation around that material. If you are still choosing a product, the estimate can identify practical installation considerations such as plank thickness, attached pad, transition needs and whether the substrate requires additional work.

What can be included in an LVP project

  • Luxury vinyl plank and vinyl plank installation
  • Whole-home or room-by-room flooring replacement
  • Removal of existing carpet or hard flooring when included in the scope
  • Subfloor inspection, preparation and localized leveling where required
  • Transitions between different flooring surfaces
  • Shoe molding or baseboard work as specified in the estimate
  • Layout planning through halls, doorways and connected living areas

Why floor preparation matters

Floating LVP needs a surface that meets the flooring manufacturer's flatness and installation requirements. Low spots, abrupt height changes, damaged substrate or leftover adhesive can create movement, visible imperfections or problems at transitions. We identify preparation needs before installation and list them separately in the scope when they are required.

For projects that need more preparation, see our floor removal and subfloor preparation page.

Whole-home vinyl flooring

For larger projects, continuity matters. We plan the direction and sequence of installation around the main sight lines, connected spaces, hallway widths and transition points. The goal is a finished floor that reads consistently from room to room rather than looking like separate isolated installs.
