Why the surface under the plank is checked

Floating LVP needs a surface that meets the flatness the manufacturer specifies. Low spots, abrupt height changes or leftover adhesive can open joints. We check the slab or subfloor at the free measure and list any leveling in the written quote before work starts.

LVP types: click-lock, glue-down and loose-lay

Luxury vinyl plank comes in a few installation formats, and they are not interchangeable. Click-lock (floating) planks lock together and float over the slab or subfloor without adhesive — this is the format in the $3.99 package, and it is the common choice for homes. Glue-down LVP is adhered to the subfloor and is more typical in commercial space. Loose-lay and peel-and-stick formats exist in the market, but they are not part of this package. Rigid-core construction and what "waterproof" actually covers are explained on the waterproof and rigid-core (SPC) LVP page.

LVP in Jacksonville homes: humidity, pets and wet rooms

Waterproof LVP suits how North Florida homes actually get used. The wear surface handles spills, pet accidents and tracked-in rain without soaking in the way carpet does, which is why so many Jacksonville households put it in kitchens, laundry rooms and hallways. The planks acclimate in your home before installation so they adjust to its temperature and humidity. One boundary to keep in mind: a waterproof floor is not a cure for an active leak. If the subfloor is wet from plumbing, that gets fixed first.

What an install day looks like

On install day the crew clears the work area (furniture moving is quoted separately at $50/room), removes the old floor when removal is on the quote, and re-checks the slab or subfloor. Any leveling listed on the quote goes in first. Then underlayment or the Plus moisture barrier, planks laid with the required expansion gap at the walls, transitions at doorways, shoe molding, and a standard cleanup. The install date is written on the quote, so there is no vague "we'll call you" window.

Keeping LVP floors looking new

Day-to-day care is simple: sweep or vacuum, then a damp mop with a pH-neutral cleaner made for vinyl floors. Skip steam mops, abrasive pads and wax-based products — heat and grit are what age a vinyl surface fastest. Felt pads under furniture legs and a rug at the front door do more for long-term wear than any special product.

Can LVP go over my existing tile floor?

Often yes, if the tile is sound and firmly bonded. Grout lines can telegraph through over time, so the installer checks flatness first — the same flatness check we do on any slab or subfloor at the free measure. Tile removal is also available and is quoted separately.

Is LVP a good choice with dogs and kids in Jacksonville?

Yes. Waterproof LVP is one of the most forgiving floors for pets and children: spills and accidents sit on the surface instead of soaking in, and the wear layer resists scratches from normal traffic. Keep nails trimmed and use felt pads under heavy furniture.

Can LVP be installed in a bathroom or laundry room?

Yes — a waterproof surface makes it a practical fit for wet-prone rooms. The subfloor must be dry and sound first, and transitions get sealed where water is likely. It is not a fix for an active leak.

What is the difference between click-lock and glue-down LVP?

Click-lock planks float over the floor without adhesive and lock together — faster to install and removable later. Glue-down LVP is adhered to the subfloor and is more common in commercial space. The $3.99 package is click-lock floating installation.

Do the planks need to acclimate before installation?

Yes. The boxes sit in your home before install day so the planks adjust to its temperature and humidity. Installing vinyl that has not acclimated is how gaps and buckling start.

How do I maintain and clean LVP floors?

Sweep or vacuum regularly and damp-mop with a pH-neutral cleaner made for vinyl. Avoid steam mops, abrasive pads and wax-based products, and put felt pads under furniture legs.

How thick is the in-stock LVP, and what is the wear layer?

The thickness and wear-layer numbers for the current in-stock lot are not published on this page — the lot changes. Both are confirmed on your written quote at the free measure.

Will LVP buckle in Florida heat?

Properly installed floating LVP handles normal seasonal movement through the expansion gap at the walls. Problems start with poor acclimation, no expansion gap, or heat sources pressed directly on the floor — all things covered at the measure.
