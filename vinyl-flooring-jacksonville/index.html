Why floor preparation matters

Floating LVP needs a surface that meets the manufacturer's flatness requirements. Low spots, abrupt height changes or leftover adhesive can cause movement and visible joints. We check the slab or subfloor at the free measure and list any leveling in your written quote before work starts. Learn more about removal and floor preparation.
