Showers in St. Johns new construction

The CR-210 subdivisions are full of builder showers: standard tile, standard layout, grout already aging. A re-tile is the chance to do the system properly — waterproofing discussed at the measure and included when it is in the agreed scope, a layout planned around the niche and bench, and grout joints sized to the tile. Mandarin: what the measure looks for in older homes

In Mandarin bathrooms, the tile question is often really a substrate question: slab condition near the river side of the house, subfloor wear around tubs, and plumbing-era walls that are not flat. None of that is visible in a photo of the room, which is why the quote comes after a measure. Prep is itemized on the quote — if the room needs nothing extra, the quote says that too.

Backsplashes, entries and floors

Backsplash is the highest-visibility small job in both areas, and layout is planned so the pattern survives the outlets and corners. Entry floors near the river get porcelain for the same reason beach houses do — water arrives daily and porcelain does not absorb it. Ceramic serves dry walls and backsplashes well; porcelain earns the floors and wet rooms. The measure confirms which body tile goes where.

Tile installation in Mandarin & St. Johns — FAQs

Is there a published per-square-foot price for tile in Mandarin or St. Johns?

No. Tile is quoted after a free measure because the price depends on the tile size, the room, demolition and the prep the surface needs. The $3.99 per square foot figure on this website is only the waterproof LVP package, which also applies in Mandarin and St. Johns.

My St. Johns home has builder tile I want to upgrade. What is involved?

Usually demolition of the builder tile in the room being redone, prep of the slab, and the new setting. Builder tile is often set over large areas quickly, so removal and surface prep are the variables — both are assessed at the measure and written into the quote.

What tile work is common in Mandarin homes?

Bathroom floors and showers in 1970s–90s homes, kitchen backsplashes, and entry floors near the river side of the house. Older Mandarin bathrooms sometimes hide dated plumbing and subfloor wear that the measure checks before tile is quoted.

Do shower projects include waterproofing?

A shower is not treated like a dry wall. Waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included only when it is part of the agreed tile scope. The specific system and any written warranty are confirmed on the quote.

Porcelain or ceramic for a St. Johns kitchen backsplash?

Ceramic is a solid backsplash choice — it is a dry wall application where hardness matters less and the style range is wide. Porcelain earns its place on floors and in wet areas. We confirm the right body tile for each surface at the measure.
