How to read these examples

Every number below comes from the published price list: the $3.99/sq ft Promo package (500 sq ft and up), Plus at $4.49/sq ft, Premium from $4.99/sq ft, the flat small-room prices under 500 sq ft, carpet removal at $0.50/sq ft, tile removal from $2.00/sq ft, the $400 dumpster when we haul debris, and the rest of the add-on list. The examples are estimates. Your written quote after the free measure is the final price, because only a measure confirms the square footage, the current floor and whether the slab needs leveling.

Square footage is the floor area being covered. Closets and hallways count. Most quotes round up for cuts and waste on the measure, so the number on your quote can be slightly higher than your own room-by-room math.

Example 1: one bedroom with carpet, about 180 sq ft

Under 500 sq ft the per-square-foot packages do not apply. A 180 sq ft room falls in the 151–250 sq ft tier: $1,195 flat for in-stock waterproof LVP, installation and cleanup. Carpet removal adds 180 × $0.50 = $90, and the dumpster is $400 when we haul the debris. Estimated total: $1,685. Moving a bed and dresser out of the room is the $50/room furniture add-on if you would rather not do it yourself.

Example 2: two bedrooms and a hall, about 420 sq ft

Still under 500 sq ft, so the 351–499 sq ft tier applies: $1,895 flat. Carpet removal: 420 × $0.50 = $210. Dumpster: $400. Two doorway transitions to tile in the bathroom and kitchen would add $35–45 each, so roughly $70–90. Estimated total: $2,575–2,595.

Example 3: living, dining and hall, 600 sq ft over carpet

At 500 sq ft and up the package price applies. 600 × $3.99 = $2,394 for the Promo package. Carpet removal: 600 × $0.50 = $300. Dumpster: $400. Estimated Promo total: $3,094. Plus, which adds new shoe molding and a moisture barrier over the slab, is 600 × $4.49 = $2,694, plus the same removal and dumpster: $3,394. On a Jacksonville slab home, Plus is often the better value than buying shoe molding separately at $2.99 per linear foot.

Example 4: 1,000 sq ft first floor that has old ceramic tile

Package: 1,000 × $3.99 = $3,990. Old tile has to come up first, from $2.00/sq ft, which adds from $2,000, plus the $400 dumpster. Estimated total: from $6,390 before any leveling. Tile removal often leaves thinset ridges and low spots, so plan for some leveling: spot leveling is $75 per bag ($150 minimum) and area self-leveling is $2.50/sq ft at a 1/4" average ($300 minimum). The full process is on the tile-to-LVP page.

Example 5: 1,200 sq ft whole home over carpet, Plus package, toilet reset

1,200 × $4.49 = $5,388. Carpet removal: 1,200 × $0.50 = $600. Dumpster: $400. LVP in a hall bathroom means pulling and resetting the toilet ($99). Four transitions at $35–45 each add $140–180. Estimated total: $6,627–6,667 (Plus shoe molding and moisture barrier included; carpet removal and dumpster separate).

Example 6: 800 sq ft over carpet with a staircase

Floor: 800 × $3.99 = $3,192. Carpet removal: 800 × $0.50 = $400. Dumpster: $400. Stairs are priced per step at $85–110, so 13 steps adds $1,105–1,430. Estimated total: $5,097–5,422. Stairs take longer than open floor, and the step price covers the extra cutting and nosing work.

What changes a quote after the measure

  • The real square footage, including closets and waste for cuts
  • What is on the floor now: carpet removal $0.50/sq ft; tile or glued floors from $2.00/sq ft; floating LVP or laminate $0.75/sq ft; dumpster $400 when we haul debris
  • Slab flatness: leveling is listed on the written quote before work starts
  • Stairs, transitions, toilet resets and furniture moving
  • Choosing a Premium plank instead of an in-stock color

Military, veterans and first responders can ask about 5% off. If you already bought your own plank, that is a custom labor quote, not the $3.99 package.

LVP Quote Examples — FAQs

Is $3.99 per square foot the final price?

It is the package price for 500 sq ft and up with an in-stock color, and it includes the LVP, installation and cleanup. Add-ons such as tile removal, leveling, stairs, transitions, toilet reset and shoe molding are listed separately. The final price is the written quote after the free measure.

What does a single room cost?

Rooms under 500 sq ft use flat prices: $895 up to 150 sq ft, $1,195 for 151–250 sq ft, $1,495 for 251–350 sq ft and $1,895 for 351–499 sq ft. Carpet removal is $0.50/sq ft and is not included; dumpster is $400 when we haul debris.

How much does it add if I have tile now?

Tile or glued-down floor removal starts at $2.00 per square foot. On 1,000 sq ft that is from $2,000 on top of the package, plus a $400 dumpster when we haul debris, plus any leveling the slab needs after the tile comes up.

Is Plus worth it over Promo?

Plus is $4.49/sq ft and adds new shoe molding and a moisture barrier. Shoe molding alone is $2.99 per linear foot, so on a slab home Plus is often the simpler choice. The measure shows which one makes sense for your floor.

How fast do I get a written quote?

After the free measure, the written quote comes within 24 hours. The install date is written on the quote.
