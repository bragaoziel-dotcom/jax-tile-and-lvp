Before we install your material

  • Have the boxes on site and unopened; we check product, color/lot and quantity before starting.
  • Order roughly 10% extra for cuts and waste (more for diagonal layouts or many small rooms).
  • Let the planks sit in the home if the manufacturer's instructions call for acclimation.
  • Tell us about underlayment, transitions and baseboard/quarter round you bought, so we know what is missing.
