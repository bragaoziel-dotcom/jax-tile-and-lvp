    Common questions

    LVP installation FAQs

    Is $3.99 per square foot really installed?

    Yes. The Promo includes waterproof LVP in our in-stock colors, installation and standard cleanup. Package price for 1,000 sq ft and up. Plus ($4.49/sq ft) adds shoe molding and a moisture barrier. Carpet removal ($0.50/sq ft), tile or glued-floor removal (from $2.00/sq ft), dumpster ($400 when we haul debris), floor leveling, transitions, stairs, toilet reset and furniture moving are quoted separately at the free measure.

    What is the difference between Promo, Plus and Premium?

    Promo ($3.99) is our in-stock waterproof LVP installed (carpet removal is $0.50/sq ft, not included). Plus ($4.49) adds new shoe molding and a moisture barrier. Premium (from $4.99) uses a thicker plank; the exact price depends on the plank you choose.

    Is LVP good with pets and kids?

    Yes. The planks are waterproof and easy to clean, so spills and pet accidents wipe up. For heavy traffic and big dogs, ask about the thicker Premium plank.

    Can you install over a concrete slab?

    Yes. Most Jacksonville homes are on slabs. We check flatness and moisture at the free measure. The Plus package includes a moisture barrier. Uneven areas are leveled for an extra charge (from $75/bag).

    How long does it take?

    You get a written quote within 24 hours of the free measure, with your install date. Because the Promo LVP is in stock, there is no waiting on special orders.

    Do I need to move the furniture?

    You can clear the rooms yourself, or we move regular furniture for $50 per room. Pianos, safes and aquariums are not included.

    Do you offer a military or first-responder discount?

    Yes — military, veterans and first responders, ask about 5% off when you book your free measure.

    What if my project is under 1,000 sq ft?

    The $3.99 package and the Plus and Premium prices are for projects of 1,000 sq ft and up. For smaller areas, call or text us and we will tell you whether we can schedule it.

    Who operates Jax Tile & LVP?

    Jax Tile & LVP is a trade name operated by Braga Remodeling, MB FLOOR AND TILE INSTALLATIONS LLC, in Jacksonville, Florida. The phone number is (904) 520-1994. This site covers a fixed-price LVP package and tile installation. Other remodeling is on bragaremodeling.com.

    Do you install tile as well as LVP?

    Yes. Tile floors, walls, backsplashes and showers are quoted after a free measure. Tile does not use the $3.99 per square foot LVP package price.
