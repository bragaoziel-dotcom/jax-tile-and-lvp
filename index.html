Common questions

Jacksonville flooring FAQs

Do you install customer-supplied LVP or tile?

Yes. We can quote installation based on your selected material and the preparation required for the existing floor. Material suitability is reviewed as part of the estimate.

Can you remove the old flooring first?

Yes. Removal and preparation can be included in the project scope for carpet, existing hard flooring and other surfaces when needed.

Do you level floors before LVP or tile?

When the substrate needs correction, floor preparation or leveling can be added to the scope before installation. The amount depends on what is found during inspection.

How do I get a flooring estimate?

Use the form below, call or text (904) 520-1994. Include the service, approximate square footage, ZIP code and what flooring is currently installed.
