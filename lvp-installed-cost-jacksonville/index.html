Worked example: 1,000 sq ft of carpet to LVP

Our package: 1,000 × $3.99 = $3,990 for material and installation. Add carpet and pad removal (1,000 × $0.50 = $500), the $400 dumpster if we haul it, and shoe molding if you want it (for example, 300 linear feet × $2.99 = $897). That comes to about $5,787 before any leveling, transitions or stairs.

The published range: 1,000 sq ft at $8–$17 runs $8,000–$17,000 all-in for the flooring, prep, vapor barrier, quarter round, transitions and furniture moving, plus carpet removal (1,000 × $0.35 = $350), per the same guide.

The gap is real, but the comparison only works if the product is comparable. Their range spans several grades, including premium plank; our $3.99 uses the in-stock waterproof colors. Ask any installer for the product name, the wear layer and the plank thickness, and compare those along with the price.

How to compare any LVP quote

  • Get the price per square foot with and without material.
  • Ask whether removal, the dumpster, quarter round, transitions and furniture moving are in or out.
  • Ask for leveling to be written as a line item before work starts.
  • Ask for stairs as a per-step price.
  • Compare the product: brand or line, wear layer, thickness and whether it is waterproof.
