Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026.

Information you provide

When you request an estimate, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, approximate square footage, current floor type, price-calculator selections and project details. Jax Tile & LVP uses this information to respond to your request, discuss the project and provide related customer service.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics and may use connected advertising measurement tools (such as Google Ads and, if enabled, the Meta Pixel) to understand website usage, measure calls or form activity and improve marketing performance. These services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and the configuration applied to this site. We do not send your name, phone, email, ZIP code or project details to these analytics or advertising tools.

Calls, texts and email

If you submit a project request, we may contact you about that request by phone, text message or email using the information you provided. Message and data rates may apply. We do not state that submitting an estimate request enrolls you in unrelated promotional messaging.

Sharing

We do not publish the personal details submitted through the estimate form. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, communications, analytics or lead handling when necessary for those services.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to reduce spam and unauthorized form submissions, but no website or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to braga@bragaremodeling.com or by calling (904) 520-1994.
