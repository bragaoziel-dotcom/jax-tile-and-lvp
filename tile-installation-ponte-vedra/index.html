Showers: the primary-suite job

The most requested tile scope here is the primary shower: larger format walls, a mosaic floor that follows the slope to the drain, a niche and a curb. A shower is a wet system, not a decorated wall — waterproofing is part of the conversation at the measure and is included when it is part of the agreed scope, with the system and any written warranty confirmed on the quote. Layout is drawn up before setting starts so niche lines and grout joints land where the eye expects them.

Large-format tile and the flatness rule

Big porcelain planks and 24-inch formats show every ridge under them and need lippage-free setting, which means the surface gets checked and corrected first. That prep is the difference between a floor that reads as one calm plane and one that catches light on every edge. It is assessed at the measure and written into the quote — the honest reason tile is not priced by the square foot over the phone.

Backsplashes and the kitchen refresh

The other Ponte Vedra staple is the backsplash that finishes a kitchen: subway, handmade-look or mosaic tile behind ranges and sinks, with outlet cuts planned so the pattern does not break at every receptacle. Backsplashes are small in area and high in visibility — measuring and layout happen in the same visit so the written quote covers the whole job.

Tile installation in Ponte Vedra & Nocatee — FAQs

Is there a published per-square-foot price for tile in Ponte Vedra?

No. Tile is quoted after a free measure because the price depends on the tile size, the room, demolition and the prep the surface needs. Large-format and natural-look porcelain in Ponte Vedra homes often needs more prep than a standard floor. The $3.99 per square foot figure on this website is only the waterproof LVP package.

What tile work is most common in Ponte Vedra and Nocatee homes?

Primary-suite showers and bathroom floors, kitchen backsplashes, and laundry and mudroom floors. In Nocatee's newer homes it is often upgrading a builder-tile shower or extending tile into rooms the builder carpeted.

Do shower projects include waterproofing?

A shower is not treated like a dry wall. Waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included only when it is part of the agreed tile scope. The specific system and any written warranty are confirmed on the quote.

Can you match the tile my builder installed so a repair blends in?

Sometimes, but matching discontinued tile is unreliable — dye lots and sizes vary between production runs. We look at the existing tile at the measure and tell you honestly whether a repair or a re-tile is the better call.

Can large-format tile go in my bathroom?

Large-format porcelain is common in Ponte Vedra primary baths, and it demands a flatter surface than small tile — the bigger the tile, the less it forgives. Flatness and any leveling are assessed at the measure and written into the quote before work starts.

Do you handle HOA or community access rules in Nocatee?

Yes. Put the community name in the project details when you send the form and we schedule measure and install days around gate access and work-hour rules.
