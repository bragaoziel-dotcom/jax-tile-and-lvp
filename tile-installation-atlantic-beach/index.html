Tile work in a beach town

Atlantic Beach mixes older beach cottages and concrete-block homes with newer infill, and the tile story is different from the inland suburbs. Floors here take sand, wet feet and humidity every day, bathrooms in the older houses still carry their original tile or layers of flooring over it, and second homes and rentals need work scheduled between guests. Most of our Atlantic Beach tile work is entryways and bathrooms that have to shrug off beach traffic, shower re-tiles in houses where the coastal air never really dries out, and kitchen floors and backsplashes in cottages being brought up to date.

Old floors out, sound tile in

Older Atlantic Beach homes often hide the real surface: sheet vinyl or laminate over the original floor, tile set decades ago, patches from old repairs. Until the layers come up, nobody knows exactly what the new tile will sit on. We remove the old flooring, check the substrate for damage and level problems, and write the prep into the quote before setting starts — the new floor is only as flat and solid as what is under it.

Showers in humid houses get re-tiled right

The most common Atlantic Beach shower complaint is grout that keeps failing in a house that stays humid most of the year. A re-tile is the moment to fix what is behind the tile, not just the surface. Waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included only when it is part of the agreed tile scope; the specific system and any written warranty are confirmed on the quote.

Condos, rentals and scheduling

Beach condominiums set contractor hours, elevator and parking rules, and some require sound underlayment under hard floors; rental and second homes add turnover windows between guests. Tell us the building or the rental calendar when you book the measure and we schedule inside those limits. Atlantic Beach tile questions

Tile installation in Atlantic Beach — FAQs

Is there a published per-square-foot price for tile in Atlantic Beach?

No. Tile is quoted after a free measure because the price depends on the tile size, the room, demolition and the prep the surface needs. The $4.99 per square foot figure on this website is only the waterproof LVP package, which also applies in Atlantic Beach.

Why do Atlantic Beach homeowners pick tile at the entry and bathrooms?

Sand, wet feet and humidity come in from the beach every day. Tile handles all three without swelling or trapping grit the way softer floors do, which is why entryways, bathrooms and kitchens are the most common Atlantic Beach tile jobs.

My Atlantic Beach home is older, with layers of flooring. Can you remove it and re-tile?

Yes. Older cottages and concrete-block homes often carry sheet vinyl or laminate over the original surface. We remove the layers, check the substrate for damage and level problems, and write the prep into the quote before the new tile is set.

My shower grout keeps failing in a humid coastal house. Is a re-tile the fix?

Usually, yes — recurring grout failure often traces back to what is behind the tile, and constant coastal humidity makes a tired surround fail faster. At the measure we look at the surround and say plainly whether a re-tile with proper waterproofing, confirmed on the quote, is the right scope.

Do condo or association rules affect a tile job in Atlantic Beach?

They can. Beach condominiums and some neighborhoods set work hours, elevator and parking rules for contractors, and underlayment requirements. Tell us the building or community when you book and we confirm the rules before the measure is scheduled.
