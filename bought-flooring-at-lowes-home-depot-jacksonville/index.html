How store installation programs work

The big stores generally do not install with their own employees. Lowe's says its installation program uses "a network of Independent Service PROviders" and starts with an in-home measurement by an independent installer, after which Lowe's prepares the quote (Lowe's Installation Program, Lowe's flooring installation). Floor & Decor says it "partners with Installation Made Easy" for professional installation (Floor & Decor installation services). The Home Depot also sells flooring installation services; check its current pricing and terms directly with the store.

These programs work well for many people. Prices vary by store and project, so we don't quote theirs here — get their written quote and compare.
