Matching — or deliberately contrasting — a builder floor

Nocatee homes usually start this conversation: the builder's wood-look floor runs through the living areas and the bedrooms are carpet. Matching a discontinued builder plank exactly is unreliable, and we will not pretend otherwise at the measure. The two honest options are a close in-stock tone that blends at the doorway transition, or a deliberate contrast that makes the bedroom floor its own choice. We lay samples of the in-stock colors against your existing floor, in your light, before you pick.

Upstairs bedrooms and sound

Second-floor carpet swaps are the other Nocatee staple. Floating LVP over a wood subfloor carries more footfall sound than carpet did, which surprises some homeowners. At the measure we talk through underlayment expectations honestly: the Plus package adds a moisture barrier, and where sound matters most — a primary suite over the living room — that conversation decides the tier.

Coastal humidity, five minutes from the ocean

Ponte Vedra homes live with salt air and high humidity, and open lanais mean rain gets tracked in. A waterproof wear surface handles that better than carpet or laminate: spills and wet footprints stay on the surface. The planks acclimate inside your home before install day so they adjust to its temperature and humidity — skipping acclimation is how gaps start, and it is part of the written process, not an upsell.

Why do so many Nocatee homeowners replace carpet that is not that old?

Production builders typically carpet the bedrooms and upstairs while the living areas get a hard floor. Within a few years the carpet shows traffic lanes while the rest of the house still looks new. Swapping it for waterproof LVP in the same tone family makes the home read as one floor — the single most common Ponte Vedra–area job we quote.

Can the new LVP match the wood-look floor my builder already installed?

Exact matches to a discontinued builder product are not guaranteed — dye lots and plank sizes change between production runs. At the free measure we compare the in-stock colors against your existing floor in your light and tell you honestly whether the match is close or whether a deliberate contrast at a transition reads better.

Do you work with Ponte Vedra and Nocatee HOA rules?

Yes. Some communities set work hours, elevator reservations for condos, or gate access for crews. Tell us the community name on the form and we plan the measure and install days around the rules instead of discovering them at the gate.

Can LVP replace the tile in my kitchen without demolition dust everywhere?

Tile removal is the dusty option, and it is quoted separately (from $2.00/sq ft). Where the existing tile is sound and firmly bonded, LVP can often float over it, which skips demolition entirely. Grout lines can telegraph through over time, so flatness is checked at the measure first — we will tell you which route your kitchen actually qualifies for.

What if my project is under 1,000 square feet?

The $3.99 package and the Plus and Premium prices are for projects of 1,000 sq ft and up. For smaller areas, call or text us and we will tell you whether we can schedule it.
