Tile work in the oldest city

St. Augustine tile jobs split into two worlds. In the historic district and the older neighborhoods — Lincolnville, North City, West Augustine — bathrooms and kitchens sit in houses built long before tile substrates were standardized. In the subdivisions and out toward World Golf Village and the beach, the work is the familiar Northeast Florida mix: shower re-tiles, backsplashes and floor replacements. The setting skill is the same; the prep is not, and the quote is written after a measure for that reason.

Historic homes: prep is the job

In a 1920s bathroom, the tile is the visible 20 percent. The rest is what is behind it: plaster that has to come off or be covered with a proper backer, framing that is out of plumb, and a floor structure that flexes. Tile set over a flexing surface cracks at the grout lines first and the tile second. At the measure we say plainly what the room needs, and the written scope lists it. If a shower is in the scope, waterproofing is discussed at the measure and confirmed on the quote — a shower is never treated like a dry wall.

Patterns that belong in these houses

Hex mosaics, penny rounds and subway tile are not retro novelties here — they are what these houses were built with, and modern porcelain versions are denser and absorb less water than the originals. For a historic bathroom that still has to work as a daily bathroom or a rental, that combination is the honest recommendation: period pattern, modern body.

Rentals and second homes

St. Augustine's rental market means many tile jobs run against a booking calendar. Tile work has built-in pauses — set, cure, grout, seal — so the quote lists the days we are in the house and the day the room returns to service. Give us the open window when you request the measure and we plan inside it.

St. Augustine tile questions

Tile installation in St. Augustine — FAQs

Is there a published per-square-foot price for tile in St. Augustine?

No. Tile is quoted after a free measure because the price depends on the tile size, the room, demolition and the prep the surface needs. The $3.99 per square foot figure on this website is only the waterproof LVP package, which also applies in St. Augustine.

Can you re-tile a bathroom in a historic St. Augustine home?

Yes, and older bathrooms need an honest look first. Plaster walls, out-of-plumb framing and old cast-iron plumbing change the prep. At the measure we check the substrate and say what has to be corrected before tile goes on — that prep is written into the quote rather than discovered mid-job.

What tile fits a historic home without looking out of place?

Small-format hex and penny rounds on floors, and subway tile on walls, are period-appropriate patterns that are still standard products. Porcelain versions give a historic pattern with a modern, low-absorption body — a practical choice for St. Augustine bathrooms that see daily use.

Do shower projects include waterproofing?

A shower is not treated like a dry wall. Waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included only when it is part of the agreed tile scope. The specific system and any written warranty are confirmed on the quote.

My St. Augustine home is a vacation rental. Can tile work fit between bookings?

Usually. Tile has cure time built in — setting, grouting and sealing happen on different days — so the written quote lists the work days and the date the room can go back into service. Tell us the property rents and give us the open window when you request the measure.
