Demolition and the slab under it

Old tile and sheet vinyl come off, and what is underneath decides the prep: a settled slab may need leveling in a low corner; old adhesive has to be dealt with before new setting material bonds. That work is listed on the written quote after the measure. It is also why we do not price Orange Park tile over the phone — two identical-looking bathrooms can need very different prep.

Tub-to-tile showers and bathroom floors

Swapping a fiberglass tub-shower for a tiled shower is the signature job in these homes. The shower is a wet system: waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included when it is part of the agreed scope, confirmed on the quote. Bathroom floors get porcelain — denser and lower-absorption than standard ceramic — with layout planned so cuts fall at walls and cabinets, not in the doorway.

Kitchens: backsplash and the sheet vinyl question

Backsplashes finish most Orange Park kitchen refreshes, and the floor question is usually the old sheet vinyl. Where vinyl is coming up anyway, porcelain tile is the long-life replacement; where the budget points the other way, the LVP package covers kitchens too. We will lay out both options honestly at the measure.

Tile installation in Orange Park — FAQs

Is there a published per-square-foot price for tile in Orange Park?

No. Tile is quoted after a free measure because the price depends on the tile size, the room, demolition and the prep the surface needs. The $3.99 per square foot figure on this website is only the waterproof LVP package, which also applies in Orange Park.

What tile work is most common in Orange Park homes?

Bathroom floor and shower re-tiles in 1970s and 80s ranch homes, kitchen backsplashes, and replacing worn sheet vinyl in kitchens and laundry rooms with porcelain tile.

Can you tile over the existing tile in my bathroom?

Sometimes — the existing tile has to be sound, well bonded and flat, and the added height has to work at the door and toilet flange. We test the bond and check heights at the measure and tell you whether tile-over-tile is honest for your bathroom or whether demolition is the better money.

Do shower projects include waterproofing?

A shower is not treated like a dry wall. Waterproofing is discussed at the measure and included only when it is part of the agreed tile scope. The specific system and any written warranty are confirmed on the quote.

Do you offer a military discount on tile work?

Military, veterans and first responders can ask about 5% off the LVP package. Tile is quoted per job — ask at the measure and we will confirm what applies to your written quote before you sign anything.
