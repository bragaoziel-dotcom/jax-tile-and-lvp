New flooring is only as good as the surface below it

Some projects are ready for installation after basic cleanup. Others need old flooring removed, adhesive residue addressed, damaged areas repaired or low spots corrected before new material can be installed properly. Those conditions are easier to plan for before installation than after a new floor has already started.

Jax Tile & LVP evaluates preparation as part of the flooring estimate. The work is tied to a new LVP installation or tile installation; we are not positioning this as a standalone demolition service.

Preparation work may include

  • Removal of existing carpet or flooring included in the approved scope
  • Removal of loose debris and installation contaminants
  • Localized surface corrections before LVP or tile
  • Floor leveling where needed for the new flooring system
  • Transition-height planning between adjacent surfaces
  • Evaluation of areas that need repair before finish flooring begins

Leveling and flatness are not the same as making a floor perfectly level

Flooring manufacturers often specify flatness requirements over a measured distance. The relevant correction depends on the new flooring product, the substrate and the condition found on site. During the estimate, we identify visible concerns and explain whether additional preparation should be included.

Why this matters for LVP

Flexible floating floors can show movement or telegraph imperfections if the substrate has abrupt changes or unsupported areas. Preparation helps the plank system perform as intended and can reduce problems at joints and transitions.

Why this matters for tile

Tile needs a stable, suitable surface. Larger tile sizes make flatness especially important because uneven substrate conditions can make consistent setting and alignment more difficult. The correct preparation depends on the existing surface and tile assembly.

Transparent preparation pricing

Preparation is listed as part of the project scope when required rather than hidden inside a generic flooring price. If leveling compound is needed, the quantity depends on the actual floor condition and is addressed during the estimate.
