Leveling vs. flatness

LVP manufacturers specify how flat the floor must be over a measured distance. The right correction depends on the slab or subfloor and what we find on site. We explain what is needed, and why, before you approve the quote.

Why this matters for LVP

Floating floors can flex or show joints if the surface has abrupt changes. Proper preparation helps the planks stay locked together and perform the way the manufacturer intends.

Tile is quoted on its own

Removing tile so LVP can go down is an add-on on this page. Installing new tile — floors, walls, backsplashes or showers — is the other specialty and is not priced from this list. See tile installation or the LVP package.
