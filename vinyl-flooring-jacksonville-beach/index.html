Sand is the honest enemy

No floor is scratch-proof against beach sand, and any installer who promises one is selling. The wear layer on the in-stock plank is built for normal traffic; the habits that protect it are a door mat, felt pads and sweeping the entry. That is also why darker, grained colors are popular at the beach — they hide the daily grit between cleanings better than pale, flat tones.

Why is waterproof LVP so common in beach homes?

Sand, salt air and wet feet are daily life a few blocks from the ocean. A waterproof wear surface means beach water and spills sit on top instead of soaking into carpet fibers or swelling a laminate core. It is also why LVP replaced carpet in so many beach rentals: turn cleanings are a sweep and a damp mop.

Will sand scratch the floor?

Sand is grit, and grit scratches any floor over time. The wear layer resists scratches from normal traffic, and the realistic defenses are a mat at the door, felt pads under furniture and regular sweeping. We would rather tell you that up front than promise a scratch-proof floor — no vinyl is.

Can LVP be installed in a Jacksonville Beach condo or beach rental?

Yes, with the building's rules checked first. Some associations require sound-rated underlayment under hard floors or set work hours. Send the rules with your request. For rentals, tell us the property rents — we schedule the install in an open window between stays whenever the calendar allows.

My beach house sits on a raised foundation. Can LVP still be installed?

Often yes. Older beach cottages are frequently raised on wood subfloors rather than slab. We check for flex, squeaks and moisture damage at the free measure; soft sections are repaired first and that repair is quoted separately on the written quote.

What if my project is under 1,000 square feet?

The $3.99 package and the Plus and Premium prices are for projects of 1,000 sq ft and up. For smaller areas, call or text us and we will tell you whether we can schedule it.
