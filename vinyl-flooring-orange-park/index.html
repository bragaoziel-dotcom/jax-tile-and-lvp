The slab conversation

Floating LVP is honest about what is under it: high spots rock, low spots flex, and both open joints over time. Older Clay County slabs often have a low corner or a crack line from settling. At the measure we run the flatness check and mark exactly where leveling is needed, if anywhere. It is priced per bag (from $75) on the written quote — a line item you can see, not a surprise on install day.

Old sheet vinyl and glued floors

Kitchens and laundry rooms in these homes often have sheet vinyl glued straight to the slab. If it is tight and smooth, a floating floor can sometimes go over it. If it is peeling or there are two layers, it comes up — glued-floor removal is from $2.00/sq ft (plus $400 dumpster when we haul) and is listed separately. The form's "existing floor" field exists so the measure is planned with the right expectations.

Living in the house during the install

Most Orange Park jobs are occupied homes, so the quote can phase the work room by room: living areas first, bedrooms after, or the reverse. Furniture moving is available at $50/room. Kids and pets are part of the planning conversation — the wear surface is waterproof and forgiving once it is down, and install days in each room are short compared to a tile job.

My Orange Park ranch home is on a slab. Anything special to check?

Two things: flatness and moisture. Many Orange Park ranches from the 1970s and 80s have slabs that have settled unevenly, and low spots telegraph through floating plank. We check flatness at the free measure; leveling is quoted separately from $75 per bag when it is needed. The Plus package adds a moisture barrier over the slab.

Can LVP go over the sheet vinyl in my kitchen?

Sometimes. Old sheet vinyl that is fully bonded and smooth can sometimes stay under a floating floor; curled, loose or multi-layer vinyl cannot. We check adhesion at the measure. If it has to come up, removal is quoted separately — glued-floor removal runs from $2.00/sq ft.

Do you offer a military discount?

Military, veterans and first responders can ask about 5% off. Mention it on the form or at the measure and we apply it to the written quote.

Can you do the house room by room so we can keep living in it?

Yes, phased installs are common when a family stays in the home during the work. Furniture moving is $50 per room when you want us to handle it, and the written quote lays out the room order before install day.
