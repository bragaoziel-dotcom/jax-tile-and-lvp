Questions

Financing questions

Does checking my options affect my credit score?

There is no impact to your credit score to check your options. Pre-qualification uses a soft credit inquiry. If you choose an offer and formally apply, the lender may perform a hard credit inquiry, which could affect your credit score.

What projects can I finance?

Flexible monthly payment options are available for waterproof LVP flooring and tile installation in Jacksonville and nearby Northeast Florida communities. Bathroom and kitchen remodeling can also be financed through the same Acorn Finance application at bragaremodeling.com/financing.

How fast is the process?

You can check your options in about 60 seconds, and you will see real offers right away. Once approved, funding is typically fast — often within a few business days. Funding times vary by lender.

Who provides the financing?

Financing is available through Acorn Finance and provided by Acorn Finance lending partners. Acorn Finance is a lending marketplace, not the lender. Jax Tile & LVP is operated by Braga Remodeling, an independent contractor that does not make credit decisions.
